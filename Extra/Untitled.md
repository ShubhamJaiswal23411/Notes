
# 📅 PROGRAM STRUCTURE (4 Weeks)

**Frequency: 4 Days / Week**

- Day 1 → Heavy Lower
    
- Day 2 → Heavy Upper
    
- Day 3 → Volume Lower
    
- Day 4 → Volume Upper
    

👉 Weeks 1–3 = progressive overload  
👉 Week 4 = **deload**

---

# 🔴 WEEK 1 (Accumulation)

### Day 1 – Heavy Lower

- Squat → **4 x 4 @ 75% (71 kg)**
    
- Deadlift → **3 x 4 @ 75% (75 kg)**
    
- Leg Press → 3 x 8
    
- Hamstring Curl → 3 x 10
    

---

### Day 2 – Heavy Upper

- Bench → **4 x 4 @ 75% (67.5 kg)**
    
- Overhead Press → 3 x 6
    
- Barbell Row → 3 x 8
    
- Triceps Pushdown → 3 x 10
    

---

### Day 3 – Volume Lower

- Squat → **3 x 6 @ 70% (66 kg)**
    
- Romanian Deadlift → 3 x 8
    
- Walking Lunges → 3 x 10 each
    
- Abs → 3 x 12
    

---

### Day 4 – Volume Upper

- Bench → **3 x 6 @ 70% (63 kg)**
    
- Incline DB Press → 3 x 10
    
- Lat Pulldown → 3 x 10
    
- Lateral Raises → 3 x 12
    

---

# 🔵 WEEK 2 (Progression)

### Increase intensity, slightly reduce reps

- Squat → **4 x 3 @ 80% (76 kg)**
    
- Deadlift → **3 x 3 @ 80% (80 kg)**
    
- Bench → **4 x 3 @ 80% (72 kg)**
    
- Volume work → keep similar but push effort
    

---

# 🟡 WEEK 3 (Peak Stress Week)

### Day 1 – Heavy Lower

- Squat → **5 x 2 @ 85% (81 kg)**
    
- Deadlift → **4 x 2 @ 85% (85 kg)**
    

---

### Day 2 – Heavy Upper

- Bench → **5 x 2 @ 85% (76.5 kg)**
    

---

### Volume Days

- Squat → **3 x 5 @ 72% (68 kg)**
    
- Bench → **3 x 5 @ 72% (65 kg)**
    

👉 Accessories reduced slightly (fatigue management)

---

# ⚪ WEEK 4 (DELOAD)

### Cut volume by ~50% and intensity

### Day 1

- Squat → **3 x 3 @ 60% (57 kg)**
    
- Deadlift → **2 x 3 @ 60% (60 kg)**
    

---

### Day 2

- Bench → **3 x 3 @ 60% (54 kg)**
    

---

### Day 3 & 4

- Light technique work only
    
- Accessories → minimal (2 sets max)
    

---

# 🔁 PROGRESSION MODEL (VERY IMPORTANT)

After Week 4:

- Add:
    
    - **+2.5 kg to bench**
        
    - **+5 kg to squat/deadlift**
        
- Restart cycle
    

---

# 🧩 Why This Program Works (for Intermediate Lifters)

- **Heavy + Volume split** → balances intensity + hypertrophy
    
- **Twice/week frequency** → optimal motor learning
    
- **Submaximal training** → avoids burnout
    
- **Deload week** → ensures recovery → supercompensation
    

---

# ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Don’t turn volume days into max days
    
- Keep **1–2 reps in reserve (RIR)** except Week 3
    
- Eat + sleep properly (this program relies on recovery)
    

---
